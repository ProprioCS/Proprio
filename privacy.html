Website Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This notice explains what information Proprio Cloud Solutions LLC (“Proprio,” “we,” “us”) collects through our website, propriocloud.com, and what we do with it.

It covers the website only. If you are a Proprio client, the data in your NetSuite account and our software is covered by your agreement with us, not by this notice.

What we collect

Information you send us

If you use our contact form, we receive the information you enter:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • your company
  • your phone number, if you give it
  • your message

If you email or call us, we receive whatever you include.

Basic technical information

Our website is a simple static site. We do not add cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, or session recording to the site. Services we rely on receive basic technical information:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes.
  • Google Fonts supplies the site’s fonts. Your browser requests them from Google, which receives your IP address and browser details.
  • FormSubmit processes contact-form submissions and may direct you to a Google reCAPTCHA spam check. These third-party services may receive technical information and use cookies or similar technologies under their own privacy policies.

How we use it

We use the information you send us to:

  • answer your question or request
  • follow up about our products if you asked us to
  • keep a record of our conversation
  • protect our site and comply with the law

We do not sell your information. We do not share it for targeted advertising. We do not use it to build profiles about you.

Who we share it with

We share information with service providers and our implementation partner to help us run the site and answer you:

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or someone’s safety, or as part of a sale or merger of our business.

How long we keep it

We keep contact-form inquiries for up to 3 years after our last contact with you, unless you become a client or the law requires us to keep them longer. Then we delete them.

How we protect it

We protect information with the measures described in our Information Security Policy. Those measures include multi-factor sign-in, encrypted company devices, and access limited to the people who need it. No system is perfectly secure, but we take this seriously.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we have about you
  • correct it
  • delete it
  • stop sending you marketing email

Email info@proprio.cloud with your request. We will respond within 45 days. We may need to confirm your identity first. We will not treat you differently for making a request.

Depending on where you live, you may have other rights under your state’s privacy law. You can use them the same way.

Children

Our website is for businesses and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we will post the new version here and update the date at the top.
